# 3.1 Azure Cost Management

## What Affects Cost in Azure

### Overview
Azure costs are based on consumption - you pay only for what you use. Understanding the factors that influence costs helps optimize spending and predict expenses.

### Key Cost Factors

#### 1. Resource Type

##### Definition
Different Azure services have different pricing models and costs.

##### Examples
- **Virtual Machines**: Charged per hour/minute of operation
- **Storage Accounts**: Charged per GB stored + transactions
- **Azure SQL Database**: Charged based on performance tier and storage
- **App Service**: Charged based on App Service Plan tier
- **Azure Functions**: Charged per execution and compute time

##### Cost Variation
The same service can have drastically different costs:
- **Basic VM**: $13/month
- **Enterprise VM**: $1,000+/month

---

#### 2. Consumption (Usage)

##### Definition
Pay based on how much you use the service.

##### Metered Services
- **Compute**: Hours of VM operation
- **Storage**: GB stored and number of operations
- **Bandwidth**: Data transferred out of Azure
- **Database**: DTUs or vCores consumed
- **Functions**: Number of executions

##### Example
```
Storage Account costs:
- Storage: $0.18 per GB per month
- Transactions: $0.004 per 10,000 operations
- Data retrieval: $0.01 per GB

Usage in a month:
- 100 GB stored = $18
- 1 million operations = $0.40
- 50 GB retrieved = $0.50
Total: $18.90
```

---

#### 3. Maintenance

##### Definition
Ongoing operational costs and management overhead.

##### Maintenance Activities Affecting Cost
- **Patching and Updates**: Time and potential downtime
- **Monitoring**: Application Insights, Log Analytics ingestion
- **Backup**: Storage space for backups
- **Disaster Recovery**: Replication and redundancy costs
- **Support Plans**: Basic (free) to Enterprise ($1,000+/month)

##### Trade-offs
- **IaaS** (VMs): Lower monthly cost, higher maintenance effort
- **PaaS** (App Service): Higher monthly cost, lower maintenance effort
- **SaaS** (Microsoft 365): Highest cost, minimal maintenance

---

#### 4. Geography (Region)

##### Definition
Azure resource costs vary by region where they're deployed.

##### Why Regions Differ
- **Local infrastructure costs**: Real estate, power, labor
- **Tax and regulatory requirements**: Local taxes and compliance
- **Demand and competition**: Market conditions
- **Currency exchange rates**: For non-USD regions

##### Example Pricing Comparison
**Standard D2s v3 VM (2 vCPU, 8 GB RAM):**
| Region | Monthly Cost |
|--------|--------------|
| **East US** | $70 |
| **West Europe** | $77 |
| **Japan East** | $84 |
| **Brazil South** | $98 |
| **Australia East** | $91 |

**Difference**: Up to 40% cost variation between cheapest and most expensive regions!

##### Considerations
- Choose region closest to users (performance) vs lowest cost region
- Data residency and compliance requirements may limit region choice
- Some services not available in all regions

---

#### 5. Network Traffic

##### Definition
Data transfer costs, especially data leaving Azure (egress).

##### Network Traffic Types

###### Inbound Traffic (Ingress)
- **Cost**: FREE
- **Definition**: Data coming into Azure from internet
- **Example**: Uploading files to Azure Storage

###### Outbound Traffic (Egress)
- **Cost**: CHARGED
- **Definition**: Data leaving Azure to internet
- **Pricing**: First 5-100 GB free per month, then tiered pricing
- **Example**: Website visitors downloading files

###### Within Azure

**Same Region:**
- **Within same Availability Zone**: FREE
- **Between Availability Zones**: Small charge (~$0.01/GB)
- **Example**: VM to Storage in same region

**Between Regions:**
- **Cross-region**: CHARGED (~$0.02/GB)
- **Example**: Data replication from East US to West US

##### Network Traffic Pricing Example
```
Monthly bandwidth:
- First 5 GB outbound: FREE
- Next 10 TB: $0.087/GB = $870
- Next 40 TB: $0.083/GB
- Over 150 TB: $0.05/GB

Heavy website with 5 TB outbound: ~$435/month in bandwidth alone
```

##### Optimization Tips
- Use Azure CDN to cache content closer to users
- Minimize cross-region data transfers
- Archive data instead of frequently accessing
- Use ExpressRoute for large on-premises transfers

---

#### 6. Subscription Type

##### Definition
Different Azure subscription types offer different pricing and discounts.

##### Subscription Types

###### Free Trial
- **Duration**: 30 days or $200 credit (whichever first)
- **Includes**: Limited free services for 12 months
- **Best for**: Testing and learning

###### Pay-As-You-Go
- **Billing**: Monthly based on actual usage
- **Flexibility**: No commitment, cancel anytime
- **Cost**: Standard retail pricing
- **Best for**: Small businesses, variable workloads

###### Enterprise Agreement (EA)
- **Commitment**: 3-year agreement
- **Discount**: Negotiated pricing (typically 20-40% off)
- **Minimum**: Usually $36,000+ annual commitment
- **Best for**: Large organizations

###### Cloud Solution Provider (CSP)
- **Through**: Microsoft partner
- **Support**: Partner provides billing and support
- **Pricing**: Partner sets pricing (often includes services)
- **Best for**: Organizations wanting managed services

###### Azure in Open
- **Commitment**: Annual license purchase
- **Discount**: ~10-15% off pay-as-you-go
- **Best for**: Organizations with existing Microsoft agreements

##### Student Subscription
- **Credit**: $100 credit
- **Duration**: 12 months
- **Requirements**: Valid student email
- **Best for**: Students and education

---

#### 7. Azure Marketplace

##### Definition
Third-party solutions and services from vendors, billed through Azure.

##### Marketplace Considerations
- **Third-party costs**: Added to Azure bill
- **License models**: BYOL (Bring Your Own License) vs Pay-as-you-go
- **Support costs**: May include support fees
- **Updates**: May have subscription costs

##### Example
```
Deploy Bitnami WordPress:
- Azure VM cost: $70/month
- Marketplace fee: $10/month
- Total: $80/month
```

## Pricing Calculator vs Total Cost of Ownership

### Azure Pricing Calculator

#### Definition
Online tool to estimate Azure costs before deploying resources. Provides detailed cost estimates for Azure services.

#### Purpose
- **Pre-deployment planning**: Estimate costs before building
- **Budget planning**: Forecast monthly/annual expenses
- **Service comparison**: Compare costs of different configurations
- **Stakeholder communication**: Share cost estimates

#### How to Use

##### Access
- **URL**: https://azure.microsoft.com/pricing/calculator/
- **No Azure account required**
- **Free to use**

##### Process
```
1. Add services to estimate
2. Configure service parameters
3. View estimated costs
4. Export or share estimate
```

#### What You Can Estimate

##### Available Services
- **Compute**: VMs, App Service, Kubernetes, Functions
- **Storage**: Blob, Files, Disks
- **Databases**: SQL Database, Cosmos DB, MySQL
- **Networking**: Load Balancer, VPN Gateway, Bandwidth
- **AI/ML**: Cognitive Services, Machine Learning
- **50+ other services**

##### Configuration Options
- **Region**: Select deployment location
- **Tier**: Basic, Standard, Premium
- **Size**: CPU, RAM, storage specifications
- **Quantity**: Number of instances
- **Usage**: Hours per month, storage amount

#### Example Calculation

##### Scenario: Web Application
```
Services:
1. App Service (Standard S1)
   - Region: East US
   - Cost: $70/month

2. Azure SQL Database (Standard S0)
   - Region: East US
   - Storage: 10 GB
   - Cost: $15/month

   - Cost: $20/month

   - Data ingestion: 5 GB

#### Features
- **Save estimates**: Create account to save
- **Share estimates**: Generate shareable link
- **Export**: Download as Excel or PDF
- **Compare scenarios**: Multiple configuration tabs
- **Support plans**: Include support costs

#### Limitations
- **Estimates only**: Actual costs may vary
- **Usage assumptions**: Based on your inputs
- **Doesn't include**: 
  - Marketplace solutions
  - Some third-party costs
  - Potential overages
  - Dev/Test pricing

### Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Calculator

#### Definition
Tool to estimate cost savings by migrating on-premises infrastructure to Azure. Compares on-premises costs vs Azure costs.

#### Purpose
- **Migration planning**: Justify cloud migration
- **Cost comparison**: On-premises vs Azure
- **ROI calculation**: Return on investment
- **Executive reporting**: Business case for migration

#### How to Use

##### Access
- **URL**: https://azure.microsoft.com/pricing/tco/calculator/
- **No Azure account required**
- **Free to use**

##### Three-Step Process

###### Step 1: Define Workloads
Input current on-premises infrastructure:
```
Servers:
- Windows/Linux servers: Quantity, specs
- Physical vs virtual servers
- Database servers
- Storage capacity
- Network bandwidth

Example:
- 20 Windows VMs (4 core, 16 GB RAM each)
- 10 TB storage
- 2 SQL Servers
```

###### Step 2: Adjust Assumptions
Refine cost calculations:
```
- Software Assurance coverage
- Virtualization coverage (VM density)
- Electricity costs per kWh
- Labor costs (IT admin salaries)
- Hardware refresh cycle
- Network costs
- Datacenter costs
```

###### Step 3: View Report
See detailed cost comparison:
```
5-Year Cost Comparison:

On-Premises Total: $500,000
- Hardware: $150,000
- Software licenses: $80,000
- Datacenter: $120,000
- IT labor: $100,000
- Electricity: $30,000
- Network: $20,000

Azure Total: $300,000
- Compute: $180,000
- Storage: $60,000
- Networking: $20,000
- Support: $40,000

Savings: $200,000 (40%)
```

#### What TCO Calculator Includes

##### On-Premises Costs
- **Hardware costs**: Servers, storage, networking equipment
- **Software licensing**: Windows Server, SQL Server, etc.
- **Datacenter costs**: Space, power, cooling
- **IT labor**: Administration and maintenance
- **Electricity**: Power consumption
- **Network costs**: ISP and bandwidth

##### Azure Costs
- **Compute**: Virtual machines and services
- **Storage**: All storage types
- **Networking**: Data transfer and VPN
- **Software**: Licenses (if not using hybrid benefit)
- **Support**: Azure support plan

##### Hidden On-Premises Costs Revealed
- Hardware refresh cycles (3-5 years)
- Backup and disaster recovery infrastructure
- Security systems and monitoring
- HVAC and facility costs
- Redundancy and high availability

#### Example TCO Report

##### Scenario: Small Business Server Migration
```

- 5 physical servers
- 2 SQL Servers

On-Premises 3-Year Cost: $180,000
- Hardware: $60,000

#### 2. Cost Alerts

##### Purpose
Get notified when spending reaches thresholds to prevent surprise bills.

##### Alert Types

###### Budget Alerts
- **Set amount**: Define monthly/quarterly budget
- **Thresholds**: Alert at 50%, 80%, 90%, 100%
- **Actions**: Email notifications, webhook actions
- **Example**: Budget $1,000/month, alert at $800 (80%)

###### Credit Alerts
- **For**: Free trial, MSDN, or credit-based subscriptions
- **Notification**: When credit depletes
- **Example**: Alert when $150 of $200 credit consumed

###### Department/Enrollment Quota Alerts
- **For**: Enterprise Agreements
- **Scope**: Department or enrollment level
- **Purpose**: Track spending against allocated budgets

##### Setting Up Alerts
```
1. Cost Management → Budgets
2. Create budget
3. Set amount: $1,000
4. Set period: Monthly
5. Set alerts:
   - 50%: Email team lead
   - 80%: Email manager
   - 90%: Email finance
   - 100%: Email all + webhook
6. Save
```

## Tags

### Definition
Tags are metadata key-value pairs applied to Azure resources for organization, cost tracking, and management purposes.

### Tag Structure

#### Format
```
Key: Value

Examples:
Environment: Production
Department: Finance
CostCenter: CC-12345
Owner: john.doe@company.com
Project: WebsiteRedesign
```

#### Characteristics
- **Key**: Up to 512 characters (128 for storage accounts)
- **Value**: Up to 256 characters
- **Limit**: Up to 50 tags per resource
- **Case**: Case-insensitive for keys, case-sensitive for values
- **Inheritance**: Can be inherited by policy, not automatic

---

### Common Tag Strategies

#### 1. Cost Tracking Tags

##### Purpose
Track costs by business dimension for chargeback and showback.

##### Examples
```
CostCenter: CC-12345
Department: Marketing
Project: Q4Campaign
BudgetCode: PROJ-2024-001
```

##### Use Case
```
Cost Analysis → Group by tag: Department
Results:
- IT: $5,000
- Marketing: $3,500
- Sales: $2,800
- Finance: $1,200
```

---

#### 2. Environment Tags

##### Purpose
Distinguish between production, development, and testing resources.

##### Examples
```
Environment: Production
Environment: Development
Environment: Testing
Environment: Staging
```

##### Benefits
- Apply different policies (prod = high availability, dev = lower cost)
- Implement auto-shutdown for non-prod
- Different backup policies
- Cost allocation by environment

##### Use Case
```
Policy: Auto-shutdown VMs tagged Environment=Development
Schedule: Weekdays 6 PM to 8 AM, All day weekends
Savings: ~60% on dev VM costs
```

##### Purpose

##### Examples

- Contact person for issues

### Applying Tags

#### Method 1: Azure Portal (Manual)
```
1. Navigate to resource
2. Click "Tags" in left menu
3. Add Name (key) and Value
4. Click "Apply"
5. Repeat for other resources
```

#### Method 2: PowerShell (Bulk)
```powershell
# Tag single resource
$resource = Get-AzResource -ResourceName "myVM"
Set-AzResource -ResourceId $resource.ResourceId -Tag @{Environment="Production"; Owner="john@company.com"} -Force

# Tag all resources in resource group
$resources = Get-AzResource -ResourceGroupName "Production-RG"
foreach ($resource in $resources) {
    Set-AzResource -ResourceId $resource.ResourceId -Tag @{Environment="Production"} -Force
}
```

#### Method 3: Azure CLI (Automation)
```bash
# Tag resource
az resource tag --tags Environment=Production Owner=john@company.com --ids /subscriptions/.../resourceGroups/.../providers/Microsoft.Compute/virtualMachines/myVM

# Tag resource group (inherit to resources)
az group update --name Production-RG --tags Environment=Production Department=IT
```

#### Method 4: Azure Policy (Enforce)
```
Policy: Require specific tags on all resources
Required tags:
- Environment (Production/Development/Testing)
- Owner (email address)
- CostCenter (cost center code)

Effect: Resources without required tags cannot be created
```

---

### Tag Inheritance

#### Default Behavior
- Tags on resource groups DO NOT automatically apply to resources
- Must be explicitly copied or enforced via policy

#### Enable Inheritance with Policy
```
Policy: Inherit tag from resource group
Tag name: Environment

Effect: When resource created, automatically gets Environment tag from parent resource group
```

## Summary

### What Affects Azure Costs
- **Resource Type**: Different services, different prices
- **Consumption**: Pay for what you use
- **Maintenance**: PaaS vs IaaS tradeoffs
- **Geography**: Region impacts pricing (up to 40%)
- **Network Traffic**: Egress charged, ingress free
- **Subscription Type**: Discounts for commitments
- **Marketplace**: Third-party solutions add costs

### Pricing vs TCO Calculators
- **Pricing Calculator**: Estimate Azure costs for new deployments
- **TCO Calculator**: Compare on-premises vs Azure for migrations
- **Use both**: TCO for migration decisions, Pricing for deployments

### Cost Management Tool
- **Cost Analysis**: Understand spending patterns
- **Alerts**: Get notified of spending thresholds
- **Budgets**: Set limits and track progress
- **Recommendations**: Act on AI-driven savings suggestions
- **Exports**: Automate reporting and analysis

### Tags
- **Purpose**: Organize and track costs by business dimensions
- **Structure**: Key-value pairs (50 tags per resource)
- **Common uses**: Department, environment, owner, project, cost center
- **Governance**: Enforce with Azure Policy
- **Cost tracking**: Enable chargeback and cost allocation

### Key Takeaways
- **Multiple factors affect cost** - understand them all for optimization
- **Plan before deploying** - use calculators to estimate costs
- **Monitor actively** - Cost Management provides real-time visibility
- **Tag everything** - enables cost allocation and optimization
- **Act on recommendations** - Azure identifies savings opportunities
- **Set budgets and alerts** - prevent surprise bills
